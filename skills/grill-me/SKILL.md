Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this design until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer. Challenge me on my design a simpler or more elegant solution exists.

Ask the questions one at a time. Don't ask the next question before you got an answer.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

Write the plan and wait for my validation before implementing it.
